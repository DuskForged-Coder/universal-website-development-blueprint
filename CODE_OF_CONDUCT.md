# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The **Universal Website Development Blueprint** is an open-source engineering project intended to encourage learning, collaboration, knowledge sharing, and constructive technical discussion.

We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, inclusive, respectful, and professional environment for everyone who participates, regardless of their experience level, background, identity, or technical expertise.

Every contributor is expected to help create a positive environment where ideas can be discussed openly and disagreements can be handled constructively.

## Expected Behavior

All project participants are expected to:

- **Show respect:** Treat everyone with courtesy, professionalism, and consideration.
- **Be constructive:** Critique ideas, implementations, and technical decisions rather than attacking individuals.
- **Communicate clearly:** Explain suggestions, disagreements, and feedback with evidence and reasoning.
- **Encourage learning:** Welcome questions, beginners, experienced developers, and different perspectives.
- **Collaborate responsibly:** Respect project guidelines, contribution processes, and the time of other contributors.
- **Respect privacy:** Never disclose another person's private information without their explicit permission.
- **Maintain professionalism:** Keep discussions focused on the project and its technical objectives.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following conduct is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately hostile communication.
- Doxxing or publishing private or identifying information without consent.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or intentionally derailing technical discussions.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or inappropriate personal conduct.
- Deliberate sharing of malicious, deceptive, or harmful content.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.
- Any other behavior that creates an unsafe or hostile environment for project participants.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and activities, including:

- GitHub issues and discussions.
- Pull requests and code reviews.
- Commit messages and project documentation.
- Community interactions related to the project.
- Other spaces where an individual is officially representing or participating in the project.

## Reporting a Concern

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the repository owner.

You can reach the project owner through the contact methods listed on the owner's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/DuskForged-Coder).

When reporting a concern, please provide relevant details where possible, such as:

- A description of the incident.
- Links to relevant discussions, issues, or contributions.
- Any additional context that may help assess the situation.

**Please do not publish sensitive personal information in public issues or discussions.**

Reports will be handled with discretion and reviewed as fairly as possible. Confidentiality will be respected to the extent reasonably possible.

## Enforcement and Consequences

Project maintainers are responsible for applying this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the nature and severity of a violation, possible actions include:

1. A private warning or request to correct behavior.
2. Removal or editing of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation.
4. Rejection of contributions that violate project guidelines.
5. Removal of access to project spaces or permanent exclusion from participation.

Maintainers will consider the circumstances, impact, and severity of each incident before taking action.

Retaliation against individuals who report concerns or participate in an investigation is prohibited.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are expected to:

- Apply these guidelines consistently and fairly.
- Review reports of misconduct responsibly.
- Avoid favoritism or conflicts of interest when handling complaints.
- Take appropriate action when violations occur.
- Protect the privacy of individuals involved wherever reasonably possible.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in this project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a respectful, collaborative, and constructive open-source community.

Thank you for helping make the **Universal Website Development Blueprint** a better place to learn, build, share, and contribute.
